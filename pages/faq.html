Got Questions?

We Have Answers

Everything you need to know about MediCore Hospital's services, policies, and care.

Appointments & Scheduling

How do I book an appointment at MediCore Hospital?

You can book an appointment through our website contact form, by calling our helpline at +1 (800) 555-1234, or by visiting our hospital reception in person. We also offer an online patient portal for registered patients.

How far in advance should I book a specialist appointment?

We recommend booking specialist appointments at least 3–5 business days in advance. For urgent matters, same-day or next-day appointments may be available depending on the department. Emergency cases are seen immediately.

Can I reschedule or cancel my appointment?

Yes, you can reschedule or cancel appointments up to 24 hours before your scheduled time without any fee. You can do this via our patient portal, by phone, or in person at reception.

Do you offer telemedicine / virtual consultations?

Yes! MediCore offers telemedicine consultations for many outpatient services. Patients can consult with doctors via secure video call from the comfort of their home. Ask about availability when booking your appointment.

Insurance & Billing

What insurance plans does MediCore accept?

MediCore accepts all major insurance providers including Medicare, Medicaid, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealth, and many others. Contact our billing department for specific plan verification.

Are payment plans available for uninsured patients?

Absolutely. MediCore is committed to making healthcare accessible. We offer flexible payment plans, financial assistance programs, and charity care for qualifying patients. Contact our financial counselors for details.

When will I receive my bill after a visit?

Bills are typically sent within 2–4 weeks after your visit, once we have processed claims with your insurance provider. You can also view and pay bills online through our patient portal.

Emergency Services

Is the emergency department open 24 hours?

Yes, our Level I Trauma Center and Emergency Department are open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year including all holidays. Call 911 for life-threatening emergencies.

What should I bring to the emergency room?

If possible, bring your government-issued ID, insurance card, list of current medications, and any relevant medical records. However, in a true emergency, just come — we will handle the paperwork later.

Visiting & Facilities

What are the visiting hours for inpatients?

General visiting hours are 10:00 AM – 8:00 PM daily. ICU visiting hours are 11:00 AM – 1:00 PM and 5:00 PM – 7:00 PM. Special circumstances may allow exceptions — please check with nursing staff.

Is parking available at the hospital?

Yes, MediCore has a multi-story parking garage with over 1,200 spaces. The first 2 hours are free. Valet parking is available at the main entrance. Accessible parking spaces are located near all entrances.

Does the hospital have a cafeteria and accommodation for family?

Our hospital has a full-service cafeteria open from 6:00 AM – 10:00 PM and vending machines available 24/7. We also have a family lounge and partnered guest accommodation nearby for families of long-term patients.

Medical Records

How can I access my medical records?

Patients can access medical records through our secure online patient portal. You may also request records by submitting a Medical Records Release form at the Health Information Management department or by calling our records office.

Can I get a second opinion from a MediCore specialist?

Absolutely. We encourage patients to seek second opinions and we provide this service. Simply contact our scheduling department, share your existing records, and we will arrange a consultation with the appropriate specialist.

Still Have Questions?

Our care team is available to answer any questions you have about your health or our services.
