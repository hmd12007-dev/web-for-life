Our Story

Healing Lives Since 1999

Founded in 1999 by Dr. William Hayes, MediCore Hospital began as a small regional clinic with a vision: to make world-class healthcare accessible to everyone regardless of background or means.

Over 25 years, we've grown into a 450-bed tertiary care facility with 30+ specialized departments, 500+ medical professionals, and a research center that has contributed to over 200 published medical studies.

Our Foundation

Mission, Vision & Values

The principles that guide every decision we make and every patient we serve.

Our Mission

To provide compassionate, evidence-based healthcare that improves the health and well-being of every individual we serve, supported by innovation and a commitment to excellence.

Our Vision

To be the region's most trusted healthcare institution — a beacon of healing, research, and education that sets the gold standard for patient-centered medical care.

Our Values

Compassion, Integrity, Innovation, Excellence, Collaboration, and Respect form the core of who we are and how we treat every patient, family member, and colleague.

Our Journey

Milestones & History

Key moments that shaped MediCore into the institution it is today.

1999

Hospital Founded

Dr. William Hayes opens a 50-bed regional clinic with a team of 12 physicians.

2005

Expansion & Accreditation

Expanded to 200 beds. Received first JCI accreditation and launched the cardiac surgery program.

2012

Research Center Opens

Launched MediCore Research Institute, partnering with three major universities.

2020

Digital Transformation

Launched telemedicine platform and AI-powered diagnostic tools. Served patients across 40 countries remotely.

2024

450-Bed Flagship Hospital

Opened new state-of-the-art wing. 500+ specialists, 30+ departments, 50,000+ patients annually.
